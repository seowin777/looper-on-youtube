Looper for YouTube Videos — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

What the extension does with your data

The extension works only on www.youtube.com, inside your browser. It adds a Loop button and a panel to the YouTube player and repeats the part of the video you choose.

It stores in chrome.storage.local on your computer only your settings (loop every video, keyboard shortcuts) and the loops you save: the video id and title, and the start, end and name of each loop. Nothing is sent to any server: there is no server of ours, no analytics in the extension, no third parties. You can export your loops to a file and import them back; the file stays on your computer.

"Copy link" puts an ordinary YouTube link with the loop times on your clipboard. It goes only where you paste it.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Website analytics

The welcome and goodbye pages on this website use Google Analytics to count installs and removals. The extension itself contains no analytics code.
